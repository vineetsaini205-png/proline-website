## What government and PSU buyers need

A charging station on a government or PSU site has to work from the day it opens, often for the public as well as for staff. The specification and the scope come from a tender or a work order, the site has its own engineers and its own supply, and every step has to be on record. You need a contractor who supplies, installs, tests and commissions the whole station to that specification, and hands it over with the test results to show it.

## What SITC covers

SITC stands for supply, installation, testing and commissioning: one contractor supplies the chargers and materials, installs them, tests the work and commissions a working station. This is what each stage covers when we do it:

| Stage | What we do |
|---|---|
| Supply | Chargers of the type and power in your specification, AC, DC or 2W/3W, sourced through our tie-ups with many charger makers, with DB boxes or an LT panel, cable and earthing materials |
| Installation | Cable trenches and cabling, the panel or DB boxes, earthing, foundations, canopies, bollards and floor marking |
| Testing | Insulation and earth resistance, protection and RCD trip tests, phase sequence and the emergency stop, with each result recorded |
| Commissioning | Power-up, configuration, the OCPP connection to the chosen software and a test charge with a vehicle |
| Handover | Test results and photos of the work for every site, and a short session for site staff on using the chargers |

We don't make chargers, so we can match your specification across brands instead of fitting it to one catalogue. Every installation follows the charger maker's manual, the Central Electricity Authority's safety regulations and the Indian Standards that apply, such as IS 17017 for EV charging systems, IS 732 for wiring and IS 3043 for earthing. For the full build, see [charging station setup](/services/ev-charging-station-setup/) and [commissioning](/services/ev-charger-commissioning/).

## Chargers for each kind of site

- **Offices and secretariats:** AC chargers for staff and official cars, and a DC charger where official fleets need quick top-ups.
- **Hospitals, colleges and universities:** AC chargers in staff and visitor parking, with a DC charger where visitors stop for a short time.
- **Residential colonies and townships:** AC chargers and light-EV charging points in shared parking.
- **Depots and transport sites:** high-power DC chargers for buses, trucks and service fleets.

Many cars sold in India take 7.2 kW on AC and some take 11 kW, so 7.4 or 11 kW AC chargers are enough where cars stay for hours. DC chargers of 30–60 kW run on a three-phase LT connection; 120–240 kW units need a high LT load or an HT connection with a transformer.

## PM E-DRIVE support at government premises

Under the central PM E-DRIVE scheme, charging stations at government premises, such as offices, residential colonies, hospitals and educational institutions, can get up to 100% subsidy on the upstream infrastructure and the charging equipment, provided the public has free access to them. Government-run public sites such as railway stations, airports, bus and metro stations, public sector ports and oil company outlets can get 80% of the upstream cost and 70% of the equipment cost ([AIR News, Sep 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/centre-issues-guidelines-for-rollout-of-72300-ev-charging-stations-under-pm-e-drive/)).

Upstream infrastructure is the power side of the station, typically the transformer, LT and HT cables, distribution boxes, breakers and protection, mounting structures, fencing and civil works ([Autocar Professional, Sep 2025](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/government-releases-guidelines-for-ev-public-charging-infra-under-pm-e-drive-128940)).

- **Who applies.** Eligible ministries and state governments appoint nodal agencies, which collect demand and send consolidated proposals through a dedicated portal. BHEL is the project implementation agency ([AIR News](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/centre-issues-guidelines-for-rollout-of-72300-ev-charging-stations-under-pm-e-drive/)).
- **When it is paid.** The subsidy comes in two tranches, and the second is released after the stations are commissioned and energised ([Autocar Professional](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/government-releases-guidelines-for-ev-public-charging-infra-under-pm-e-drive-128940)).

So the subsidy runs through your department's nodal agency rather than through the installation contractor, and it is paid as the scheme's conditions are met. What we can do is survey the site and set out the chargers, the load and the electrical and civil scope for your team to use in its proposal or tender, then carry out the SITC work once it is awarded.

## When a tender asks for OEM authorisation and past work

Charging-station tenders often ask for more than a price. SBI's October 2026 tender on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) for 19 charging stations in Uttar Pradesh, for example, asked bidders for an authorisation from a charger maker, a minimum number of years in HT/LT electrical work and completed EV charging projects of a set value ([tender notice](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/39129/51516783/NOTICE+INVITING+TENDER20261003063932872.pdf)).

On tenders like these, we work with the charger maker or the main contractor that bids, as the team that surveys, installs, tests and commissions the chargers on site. If you are bidding for government or PSU charging work, share the tender and the site list early, so there is time to survey the sites and agree our scope before the bid is due.

## Power, software and records

- **The electricity connection.** The connection or load increase is taken from the DISCOM in your department's name. Our survey tells you the load to apply for and what to prepare, and we plan the installation around the date the supply will be ready.
- **Software for public chargers.** Chargers open to the public need software to start sessions, take payment and show their status. We commission chargers onto the CMS your operator uses, over OCPP, or onto [Proline CMS](/cms/): software you own, with no commission on charging payments, on your own server or hosted by us in India with 24/7 support.
- **Records.** A survey report before the work starts, and test results with photos at handover, for every site.
