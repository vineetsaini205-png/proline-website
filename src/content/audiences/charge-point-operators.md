## Many sites, many contractors, many formats

Your network team signs sites faster than your projects team can build them. The sites are spread across cities and states, each with its own site host, DISCOM and local contractor, and every contractor works a little differently. One survey misses the earth. Another gets the cable route wrong. A DC charger reaches a site whose supply isn't ready and waits there for weeks. Handover photos arrive in five formats, and a charger that powers up never shows on your CMS.

What a rollout needs is one partner that works to your checklist in every city, tells you early when a site isn't ready, brings every charger online on your CMS and hands each site over in your format.

## What we do for charge point operators

We take a site from survey to a charger that is live on your CMS, with one team for the electrical and civil work.

- **Site surveys (recce).** Supply and sanctioned load, space in the panel, the cable route, earthing, civil work, mobile signal where the charger will stand, and photos, all recorded in your survey format. See [site survey](/services/site-survey/).
- **AC and DC installation.** From 3.3 kW AC points to 240 kW DC chargers of any brand: the LT panel or DB box, a breaker and protection for each charger, and armoured cable in trenches or on tray, all to the charger maker's manual. See [DC fast charger installation](/services/dc-fast-charger-installation/).
- **Earthing.** Dedicated earth pits, tested for resistance, with the reading recorded for every site.
- **Civil work.** Plinths with cable-entry pipes, bollards, canopies and floor marking, built by the same crew. See [civil work](/services/ev-charging-civil-work/).
- **Commissioning.** Insulation, earth and protection tests, power-up, the OCPP connection to your CMS and a test session. See [commissioning](/services/ev-charger-commissioning/).
- **Chargers, if you need them.** We install the chargers you send to site, or source them for you through our tie-ups with many makers, including white-label units in your own brand.
- **Solar and storage.** Solar panels and batteries for sites that need them, such as a solar canopy over the charging bays.

## Rollouts across many cities

On a rollout, consistency matters more than any single site. This is what passes between your team and ours at each stage:

| Stage | You give us | You get back |
|---|---|---|
| Survey | Site address, site contact, planned chargers | A survey report in your format: supply, load, cable route, earthing, civil scope, photos |
| Installation | The approved layout, chargers delivered to site | Cabling, panel or DB box, earthing, foundation and mounting, to the maker's manual |
| Commissioning | OCPP backend URL and charge point IDs | Tests, power-up and a test session that shows in your CMS |
| Handover | Your handover template | Test results, serial numbers and photos, filled in your format |

- **One checklist everywhere.** The same checklist and report format in every city, so a site in one state is documented exactly like a site in another.
- **Survey before dispatch.** We flag sites where the supply, the load or the host's permission isn't ready, so chargers aren't sent to sites that can't be powered.
- **Build for the next charger.** Where you plan more chargers later, we lay spare pipes in the trench and leave spare ways in the panel, so the site isn't dug up twice.
- **One point of contact for each site**, from survey to handover.

## Onto your CMS, or ours

Most networked chargers talk to their charge management system (CMS) over OCPP. During commissioning we set each charger's backend URL and charge point ID as you give them, update the firmware if you or the maker ask, and run a test session that you can see in your CMS before the site is handed over.

If you don't run a CMS yet, or want one that doesn't take a share of every session, [Proline CMS](/cms/) is our own. It is software you own, built in your brand and handed over, with no commission on charging payments and no fee per charger per month. It connects chargers of any brand over OCPP 1.6J, and runs on your own server or is hosted by us on servers in India with 24/7 support.
