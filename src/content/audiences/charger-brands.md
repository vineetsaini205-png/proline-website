## Why installation decides how buyers see your charger

You sell chargers to buyers across India, and every unit has to be installed where the buyer is. Your own service team can't reach every city, so the job often goes to the nearest electrician, and each one works their own way: a cable too thin for the charger's current, the wrong type of RCD, an earth that was never tested. The faults that follow land on your support line and your warranty, and the buyer blames the charger, not the installer.

What you need is one installation partner that fits every unit the way your manual says, in every city it works in, and sends you proof that it did.

## What we do for charger brands

- **Check the site before the visit.** We ask the buyer for photos of the meter, main DB, electricity bill and parking spot, so a low sanctioned load, a missing earth or a long cable run shows up before the installation date, not on it.
- **Install to your manual.** Cable size, breaker rating, RCD type, earthing, mounting height and clearances are set to what your manual asks for, whether the unit is an AC wall box, a DC charger or a 2W/3W charger. See [EV charger installation](/services/ev-charger-installation/).
- **Do the electrical work around the charger.** A dedicated circuit, a DB box with the protection you specify, a tested earth and, where there is no wall, a pedestal on its own foundation.
- **Commission it.** Power-up, configuration, app pairing or the OCPP connection, and a test charge with the buyer's vehicle. See [commissioning](/services/ev-charger-commissioning/).
- **Show the buyer how to use it.** A short session at handover, so the first questions don't all reach your support line.
- **Report back.** A handover report with photos and test results for every unit, in your format.

For wall boxes in houses and housing societies, see [home EV charger installation](/services/home-ev-charger-installation/) for how we handle long basement cable runs and society permissions.

## Handover reports your warranty team can use

A charger fault is easier to settle when you can see how the unit was installed. Each report records:

- the charger model and serial number, with a photo of its label
- photos of the mounting, the DB box and its breakers, the earth and the cable route
- test results: earth resistance, insulation and the RCD trip test
- the settings used at commissioning, such as the current limit
- the buyer's name, the site address and the date of installation

If your warranty terms need more, such as a photo of the incoming supply or the buyer's signature, add it to your format and our technicians fill it in.

## Selling your chargers through Proline

Buyers often ask us which charger to buy before they have chosen a brand: home owners after buying an EV, businesses and housing societies adding chargers, operators planning DC fast charging, and fleets that need depot charging. When your chargers fit a buyer's site, we put them in front of that buyer, compared fairly on features, warranty and service, and our technicians install and commission every unit to your manual.

How brands join:

1. **Send your range.** Models, specifications, connectors, software (app or OCPP), certificates and warranty terms.
2. **Agree how it works.** Terms, warranty claims and spares are agreed directly with your team; price lists stay private.
3. **Training and demo units.** Your team trains our technicians on installation and commissioning; a demo unit helps us show buyers.
4. **Selling and installing.** We recommend your chargers where they fit, then install, commission and hand over each one.

We stay brand-neutral: we recommend a charger when it is the right fit for the buyer's site and budget. If your factory also makes [white-label chargers](/chargers/white-label-chargers/) for other companies, tell us.
