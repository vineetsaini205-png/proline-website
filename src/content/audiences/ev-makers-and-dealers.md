## What EV makers and dealers need

Every EV you sell needs somewhere to charge, and buyers judge your brand by how easy that is. They expect the home charger to be fitted around the time the vehicle is delivered. Your dealers need chargers to keep display, demo and stock vehicles charged, and to send customer vehicles home charged from the workshop. In Delhi, the EV Policy 2026 now asks for a public charging station at every dealer as well.

Doing all of this with a different local electrician in every city leads to uneven work, and to complaints that reach your dealers and your customer care team. You need one installation partner across your dealer network, working to your standard.

## Delhi EV Policy 2026: a public station at every dealer

Delhi's EV Policy 2026 asks every vehicle maker operating in Delhi to set up at least one public charging station at each of its dealers, with at least three charging points for two- and three-wheelers and two for cars ([Daily Pioneer, July 2026](https://www.dailypioneer.com/news/slug-lite/delhi-notifies-ev-policy-2026-subsidies-within-60-days?year=2026)). For a maker with many dealers in Delhi, that is a rollout across many sites, and each site needs the same things:

- **Bays the public can reach.** Charging bays where visitors can drive in and park, not behind the service gate.
- **Power for the chargers.** Enough sanctioned load, or a separate connection for the station. The dealer or your company applies to the DISCOM; our survey tells you the load to apply for and what to prepare.
- **Chargers for the vehicles you sell.** Light-EV chargers for two- and three-wheelers, and AC or DC chargers for cars.
- **Software for public use.** A way for drivers to start a session and pay: your own app or CMS over OCPP, or [Proline CMS](/cms/), where drivers scan a QR code and pay by UPI into your own account.

The same policy plans a single-window facility to help charging operators get approvals and electricity connections faster ([Daily Pioneer](https://www.dailypioneer.com/news/slug-lite/delhi-notifies-ev-policy-2026-subsidies-within-60-days?year=2026)). Check the notified policy and any guidelines issued under it for the timeline and the technical details, and we plan each dealer site to match.

## Choosing chargers for a dealership

| Where | What usually fits | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Display, demo and stock cars | 7.4 or 11 kW AC | Many cars sold in India take 7.2 kW on AC and some take 11 kW, so for most cars a 22 kW AC charger adds load but not speed |
| Workshop | AC chargers, plus a 30–60 kW DC charger | Customer cars leave charged after a service; a 30–60 kW DC charger runs on a three-phase LT connection |
| Two- and three-wheeler dealers | Light-EV chargers (Type 6 DC, Type 7 AC and DC) or socket points | Matched to the connector your vehicles use |
| Public station in Delhi | At least three points for two- and three-wheelers, two for cars | The minimum in Delhi's EV Policy 2026 |

Our advice on charger brands is free. If you supply your own chargers, we install them to your manual.
