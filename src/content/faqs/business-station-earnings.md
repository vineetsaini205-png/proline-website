question: "How much can an EV charging station earn?"
answer: "It depends on how many hours a day your chargers are in use. A station earns the gap between what drivers pay, about ₹8–15 per kWh at public AC chargers and ₹15–25 at DC chargers ([Statiq, Oct 2026](https://statiq.in/blog/2026/10/12/public-ev-charging-cost-india/)), and what each unit costs you on the EV tariff, such as ₹4.50 a unit in Delhi or ₹7.70 in Uttar Pradesh ([BEE state-wise EV tariffs](https://beeindia.gov.in/sites/default/files/annexure-A.pdf)). Rent, software and upkeep come out of that gap, and usage at a new station typically builds over 6–12 months ([Bolt.Earth](https://bolt.earth/franchise)). The [earnings calculator](/tools/ev-charging-station-earnings-calculator/) shows a quiet, likely and busy month for your state, chargers and price."
