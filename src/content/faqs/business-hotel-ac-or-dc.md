answer: "Match the charger to how long vehicles stay. Hotel guests, office staff and residents park for hours, so 7.4 or 11 kW AC chargers suit them and need far less power than DC. A DC fast charger earns its place where drivers stop for under an hour, such as a highway hotel or restaurant, and a mall can use both: AC for long visits and DC for quick top-ups.
