question: "Do you have chargers for e-scooters and e-rickshaws?"
answer: "Yes. Light EVs have their own connectors under India's charging standard: Type 6 (IS 17017-2-6) for DC charging, and Type 7, or LECCS (IS 17017-2-7), which takes both AC and DC charging through one inlet. Many e-scooters and e-rickshaws still charge through the charger that came with the vehicle, plugged into a socket, so a charging point can be a set of sockets on their own circuit with the right protection. We help you choose the right setup for your vehicles and install it; see [2W and 3W chargers](/chargers/2w-3w-chargers/)."
