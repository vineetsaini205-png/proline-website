question: "Is there a government subsidy for EV charging stations?"
answer: "The central PM E-DRIVE scheme supports public charging, but its subsidies are approval-based, not automatic ([ChargeZone](https://www.chargezone.co.in/franchise)). Proposals go through government nodal agencies: government premises such as offices, hospitals and educational institutions can get up to 100% support if they offer free public access, and busy places such as railway stations and airports can get part of the cost ([AIR News, Sep 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/centre-issues-guidelines-for-rollout-of-72300-ev-charging-stations-under-pm-e-drive/)). Plan your station so it pays without a subsidy, and check your state's EV policy as well."
