question: "Do I need a licence to open an EV charging station?"
answer: "No electricity licence is needed. Under the Ministry of Power's 2024 guidelines, setting up and running a charging station is a de-licensed activity, though each station needs a separate metering arrangement ([Mercom, Sep 2024](https://www.mercomindia.com/ministry-of-power-releases-ev-charging-infrastructure-guidelines)). You still need an electricity connection from your DISCOM, the right to use the land and any local permissions your site needs. We tell you the load to apply for and the documents to keep ready."
