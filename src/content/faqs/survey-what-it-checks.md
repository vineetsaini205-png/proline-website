question: "What does the site survey check?"
answer: "An engineer checks your supply and sanctioned load, single or three phase, and whether an LT connection is enough or HT is needed. They look at your existing wiring and earth, space for a DB box or panel, the cable route and its length from the meter or transformer to each charger, and the parking layout. They also note the civil work your site needs, such as foundations, trenches or a canopy, so you know what the job involves before you buy a charger."
