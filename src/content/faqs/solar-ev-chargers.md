question: "Can solar panels run my EV chargers?"
answer: "Partly. Rooftop or carport solar can supply your chargers in the daytime, with the grid covering the rest and the night; to use solar power after sunset you also need battery storage (BESS). For public stations, the 2024 central guidelines suggest an EV tariff 30% cheaper in solar hours and 30% dearer outside them ([Lok Sabha answer, Aug 2025](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/2994845/1/AU3099_jxi0H3.pdf)), so daytime charging can cost less even from the grid where your state applies it. We supply and install the solar system and plan it around your chargers; see [solar EV charging](/solar-ev-charging/)."
