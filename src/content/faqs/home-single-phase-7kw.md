question: "Can I install a 7.4 kW charger on a single-phase connection?"
answer: "Yes. A 7.4 kW charger is made for single phase and draws 32 A, enough for the full AC charging speed of the many cars in India that accept 7.2 kW. Whether your home can take it depends on your sanctioned load and what else runs while you charge, such as air conditioners and a geyser. If the load is tight, you can apply for a load increase, set the charger to a lower current, or choose a charger with load management; see [AC and home chargers](/chargers/ac-chargers/)."
